Where each number comes from. The customers, the tags and the orders are read from Shopify, so they are the real records. "Got in" counts customers carrying the 2k-unlocked tag, which is added when someone types the right word on a browser that already knows their email because they joined the queue. Anyone who gets in on a secret link, in a different browser, or without having joined is not counted there. Orders are matched by customer and counted inside the date range you chose, so the money always matches the people above it. The combination table follows the dates but ignores the "Got in" filter, so its unlock rate stays comparable across groups.
